\title{Resolution of staleness in order book with sublinear market order sweep}
\author{Axiomatch Labs}
\date{September 4, 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
    In this article, we propose a resolution of staleness algorithm for order books supporting sublinear market order sweep. We prove the correctness of the algorithm. Our algorithm needs $N+1$ storage, where $N$ is the number of price ticks. We prove that it is the optimal storage by showing that using $N$ storage is impossible to resolve the staleness.
\end{abstract}
